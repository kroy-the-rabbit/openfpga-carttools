Copy the 3 folders (Cores/, Platforms/, Assets/) to the root of your Analogue
Pocket SD card.

Note: macOS Finder replaces folders instead of merging them, so do it all
manually and be careful.

CartTools reads the cartridge in the Pocket's slot and writes its dumps to the
SD card, under /Assets/carttools/common/. It does not play ROMs.

Save restore is alpha and writes cartridge save RAM. Hardware restore is
verified on Pokemon Silver, Dragon Warrior III, Shadowgate Classic and Oracle
of Seasons. It requires prepared RESTORE.sav and
RESTORE.meta inputs, preflight checks, a verified recovery backup and a
three-second A hold. Keep the PRE*.sav recovery files.

Read the restore guide before preparing an input:
https://github.com/kroy-the-rabbit/openfpga-carttools/blob/main/docs/SAVE-RESTORE.md

GB flash cartridge programming is experimental. It erases and writes a
Ferrante Crafts flash cartridge (SST39SF010A) with a 32 KiB image and drives
cartridge connector pin 31, the Game Boy audio input, as a digital output.
Read the guide first:
https://github.com/kroy-the-rabbit/openfpga-carttools/blob/main/docs/FLASH.md
