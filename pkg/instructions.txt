Copy the 3 folders (Cores/, Platforms/, Assets/) to the root of your Analogue
Pocket SD card.

Note: macOS Finder replaces folders instead of merging them, so do it all
manually and be careful.

CartTools reads the cartridge in the Pocket's slot. It does not load ROMs from
the SD card; it writes them to it, under /Assets/carttools/common/.

Save restore is alpha and writes cartridge save RAM. Hardware restore is
verified on Pokemon Silver only. It requires prepared RESTORE.sav and
RESTORE.meta inputs, preflight checks, a verified recovery backup and a
three-second A hold. Keep the PRE*.sav recovery files.

Read the restore guide before preparing an input:
https://github.com/kroy-the-rabbit/openfpga-carttools/blob/main/docs/SAVE-RESTORE.md
